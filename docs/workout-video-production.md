# Nourish realistic workout animation brief

## Reference look

Use `public/assets/workouts/wall_sit.gif` as the visual reference. Match the same adult man, maroon shirt, black shorts, white shoes, warm indoor room, plant at left, exercise mat, and shelf at right. Keep a fixed camera and show the full movement without cropping hands or feet. These are exercise prototypes; a qualified reviewer must check movement before any clip is presented as form coaching.

## Deliverables

Replace the seven original schematic exercise clips below. Incline push-up now has a four-pose realistic sprite prototype in the app; it still needs motion and form review. Produce a short silent loop for each remaining exercise, plus a still poster. Use the exercise ID as the filename. Prefer browser-ready MP4 (H.264) with a WebM version if practical. Keep the first and last poses similar for a smooth loop. Do not add text, speech, logos, weights, or extra equipment. Keep written instructions in the app.

| Exercise ID          | Movement to show                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `incline_pushup`     | Hands on a stable fixed countertop or wall, body straight, elbows bend and press back to the start. No movable table. |
| `reverse_snow_angel` | Face down on the mat, arms sweep gently from sides toward overhead within a comfortable range, then return.           |
| `side_plank_knees`   | Forearm under shoulder, knees bent on mat, hips lift into a short hold and lower with control.                        |
| `cat_cow`            | On hands and knees, gently alternate back rounding and extension, then return to neutral.                             |
| `hip_mobility`       | Standing with stable support, controlled hip circles and small leg swings.                                            |
| `shoulder_mobility`  | Standing, gentle shoulder rolls and small controlled arm circles.                                                     |
| `walk`               | Comfortable walking pace in the same room, showing a few natural steps with no sprinting or strenuous motion.         |

## Review before integration

Check that each clip shows the named exercise, the body and surroundings remain consistent between frames, motion is continuous, and the loop does not invent or distort limbs. Check the movement against the written form cues with a qualified fitness professional. Keep any rejected clip out of the player and use the written instructions. The app should continue to offer pause and reduced-motion behavior.
